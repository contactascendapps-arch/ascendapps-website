Privacy Policy

Ascendapps Limited. Last updated 20 November 2026.

Ascendapps Limited, official Able2 partner NZ, builds and looks after apps, websites and AI receptionists for New Zealand businesses and organisations. This policy explains how we handle personal information we collect through our website, our own apps and our services. We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

  • Contact and business details you give us, for example when you request a demo, send us an enquiry or become a client.
  • For apps we publish under our own name: account details and basic technical information, such as your device type and app version.
  • Payment information. Payments are handled by our payment providers. We never see or store your full card details.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a demo.
  • To provide our services, and to build, set up and support your app, website or AI receptionist.
  • To send you updates you have asked for.
  • To process payments and meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Trusted service providers who help us run our business, including our platform provider Able2, our website host, Google Workspace and our payment providers. Some of them store information outside New Zealand. We take reasonable steps to make sure it is protected.
  • Anyone we are required to share it with by law.

Apps, websites and receptionists we set up for clients

Apps, websites and AI receptionists we set up for clients run under the client's own business name. Each client is responsible for the personal information they collect from their own customers and callers, and has their own privacy policy.

How we keep it safe

We use secure systems and limit who can access personal information. We keep it only as long as we need it, then delete it securely.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email us and we will reply within 20 working days.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version and its date will always be on this page.
