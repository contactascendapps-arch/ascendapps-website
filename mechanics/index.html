How a job runs with your app

Your customers never need to download anything. Only you and your team use the app.

Your customer
  1. Books a service on your booking page, or calls and your receptionist books it.
  2. Gets the quote for any extra work on their phone and taps approve.
  3. Pays from a link or your QR code, and picks up the keys.
Your team
  1. Sees today's bookings and each car's history before it rolls in.
  2. Fills in the inspection checklist on a phone at the hoist.
  3. Sends the quote, gets the yes, and finishes the job.

Common questions

Do I keep my phone number?

Yes. Customers keep calling the same NZ number they always have.

What happens if it can't answer a question?

It takes a message and sends it to you straight away, so you can call the customer back.

Can it tell people what a job will cost?

Yes. It gives quotes for the services you set up with us. Anything unusual is passed straight to you with the details.

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. Only you and your team use the app. Your customers just ring your number like they always have.

How do customer payments work?

Customers pay by link or QR code, and the money goes straight into your own Stripe account. Payments carry a 2% platform fee.

Is there a contract?

There's a minimum six month term. After that it carries on month to month.

Does it replace my accounting software or parts supplier?

No. It works alongside them. It handles your phone, bookings, quotes, payments and customer records.

How are customers reminded about bookings?

By email and push notification, not text.

Who sets it all up?

We do. We set up your receptionist and your workshop app with your name, logo, services and prices, connect your number, and look after it from there. You don't build anything.
