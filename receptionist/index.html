FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I keep my own phone number?

Yes. Your calls forward to your receptionist, so customers ring the same number they always have.

Will it sound like a robot?

No. It speaks naturally, answers in your business name and keeps the conversation moving, the way a good receptionist would.

Who sets it up?

We do, from start to finish. We set up your services, hours, prices, questions and bookings, test it with you, and look after it after it's live.

Where do I see my calls and bookings?

In your app. You get a notification for every booking and message, plus a summary and full transcript of every call.

What if I use all my minutes?

Minutes reset each month and don't roll over. Need more? You can move up a plan any time, and we'll sort it for you. We keep an eye on your usage too, so you're never caught short.

Is there a contract?

There's a one-off NZ$1,200 + GST setup and a 6 month minimum term, then it carries on month to month.

What if a call needs me personally?

It takes a proper message with the caller's details and what they need, and sends it to your app straight away.
