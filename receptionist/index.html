  • You run a call centre. It's built for one business and its own callers.

FAQ

Questions we get asked.

Something else on your mind? Get in touch and we'll talk it through.

Do I keep my own phone number?

Yes. You forward calls from your own number to your receptionist when you can't answer. Your number stays yours, and callers keep ringing the number they know. While forwarding is on, unanswered calls go to your receptionist instead of your voicemail.

What do I have to do?

Tell us about your business: what you do, your hours and what callers usually ask. We set up your receptionist, test it with you, and show you how to forward your calls.

Will callers know it's AI?

Yes. Your receptionist says it's an AI assistant when it answers, in your business name.

Where do I see my calls and messages?

In the app on your phone, with a push alert for each one. Every call comes with a summary and a written copy of what was said.

What does it cost?

A one-off setup fee of NZ$1,200, then a monthly plan: Business Lite NZ$449 (300 minutes), Business NZ$649 (500 minutes) or Business Max NZ$1,199 (1,200 minutes). Minimum 6 month term. All prices in NZ dollars.

What if I use all my minutes?

That means it's answering a lot of calls you would have missed. We keep an eye on your usage and move you up a plan when you need it. Minutes reset each month and don't roll over.
