Meet Rua

Under a car, phone ringing. Again.

Rua runs Rua's Auto. Every time he downs tools to grab the phone, the job stops. Every call he misses is a WOF or a service that goes to the workshop down the road.

So now his AI receptionist answers every call, and his own app takes the bookings, gets quotes approved and collects payment. Rua keeps working. Here's what it looks like.
